# 🍽️ Tom’s Full Cooking Prompt

I like my recipes to follow a consistent, high-quality format that supports **flexible patio-style cooking**, **interactive or solo prep**, and **great leftovers**. Here's how every recipe should be structured:

---

## 🔧 Equipment Assumptions

- **Grill**: Recteq Bullseye Deluxe pellet grill  
- **Bread Maker**: Zojirushi Virtuoso  
- **Oven/Range**: Standard indoor kitchen setup  
- **Griddle/Plancha**: Cast iron preferred; no Recteq sear kit

---

## 🗂️ Recipe Format (Standard)

Each recipe must include the following sections in this order:

### 🧂 1. Ingredients

- Metric (**grams**) **and** imperial units  
- All components listed before instructions

---

### 🔥 2. Recipe Instructions

- Step-by-step, tailored to my equipment  
- Include specific cooking setup (e.g., indirect heat on Recteq)  
- Use Kenji-level clarity and Roy Choi-style flavor

---

### ⏱️ 3. Cooking Schedule

- Assume **dinner at 6:00 PM**  
- Maximize **prep earlier in the day** to allow flexibility  
- Include a **“hold” strategy** in case my wife is late

---

### 🍱 4. Leftovers

- Built-in ideas for how to repurpose leftovers creatively  
- Should feel intentional and satisfying

---

### 🍷 5. Dessert & Drink Pairings

- Recommend complementary desserts and drinks  
- No recipes needed unless I ask for them

---

### 🧑‍🍳 6. Experience Options

Offer two cook styles:

- **Interactive Cook**: A shared, fun process with my wife  
- **Solo Cook**: Minimal ask of her, I handle most of the prep

---

### 🚀 7. Next-Level Upgrade

Optional sidebar section to elevate the dish:

- Ingredient upgrades  
- Advanced prep (e.g., sous vide, fermentation)  
- Layered sauces  
- Restaurant-style plating  
- Aromatic finishes  
- Optional amuse-bouche or composed starters
